"Politics from zero" is a structured path designed to guide you step-by-step from foundational concepts to core governing institutions and fundamental rights. Each page introduces a key topic in clear, plain language, exploring how it operates, why it matters, and where honest disagreements lie. Whether you read sequentially from beginning to end or consult individual topics on demand, this sequence equips you with the vocabulary and framework needed to navigate political discussions and analyze news critically.
