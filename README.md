# Shahrvand

A free, open-source, bilingual (English and Persian) site that teaches
political literacy: how politics works, the ideas and vocabulary behind it,
and how to read political news critically.

Shahrvand (شهروند) means "citizen". Status: just started. No content or site yet; the structure is proposed in the
issues.

## How it will read

- **Learning paths**: an ordered route from the first page to the last.
- **Standalone pages**: every topic also works alone, found by search or link.
- **Two languages**: each English page has a Persian twin.

## Editorial principles

- Fair and sourced: positions are explained in terms their own holders would
  accept; fact and opinion are kept apart; sources are cited and current
  claims are dated.
- The site's one stated starting point is basic human rights and the rule of
  law. Beyond that it does not argue for any party or side.
- Nothing is invented: an unverifiable fact, quote or statistic is left out or
  marked unverified.
